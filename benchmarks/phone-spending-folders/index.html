Which folder does a chat about phone spending go in?

A simple task with a surprising result. We asked two decision models to file a chat into one of the user's folders, 10 times per case, then changed one thing at a time to find out why one of them kept missing.

The task

Suggest a folder for a chat

A chat app keeps conversations in the user's folders and suggests where each new one belongs. Each call sends one conversation and one question through the Decisions API: “Which of the user's folders does a conversation with these messages belong in?” The options are the user's three folders and None of these. The model gives each option a probability; its pick is the highest.

Every card below is one conversation, the folders it was filed against, and one bar per model: how the model split its probability across the options, averaged over 10 runs, and how many runs picked the right folder.

What we learned

Conclusions

  1. The folder's name matters more than the language. Luna finds the folder when its name is the word the user wrote: Bills for “phone bills”, 50/50. When the folder is called Facturas or Invoices it mostly says none fits (10/50 and 10/50), even in English.
  2. jev never picked a wrong folder, but its confidence shows the same pull. It gives Bills 100%. Invoices, a word the user never said, drops to 58%–67%, and climbs back to 84%–88% once the assistant's reply says “invoices”. The word moves jev's confidence, not its answer. In Spanish, where Facturas is the everyday word, it stays at 91%–98%.
  3. Without the folder's word, Luna struggles even with an obvious match. When the user says “phone service”, Luna gets Bills 40/50 and Invoices 0/50.
  4. The carrier is not the cause. With Verizon and T-Mobile instead of Personal Flow, Luna leans further towards the Personal folder, not less.
  5. For this feature, a folder suggestion should not depend on the user typing the folder's exact name. In this test, only jev met that bar.

Limits

  • One topic, hand-written messages, one wording of the question, three folders. It shows a pattern, not a general ranking of the two models.
  • jev varies between runs, so a rerun will not give exactly these numbers.
  • experimental_decide is experimental in the AI SDK, and the Decisions API is in beta.
