The task

Suggest a folder for a chat

A chat app keeps conversations in the user's folders and suggests where each new one belongs. Each call sends one conversation and one question through the Decisions API: “Which of the user's folders does a conversation with these messages belong in?” The options are the user's three folders and None of these. The model gives each option a probability; its pick is the highest.

For comparison, two text models, GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5, get the same question and options as instructions and answer with one option. They return no probabilities, so their bars show how the 10 runs split. On this page “Luna” alone means GPT-6 Luna Decisions.

Every card below is one conversation, the folders it was filed against, and one bar per model: how the model split its probability across the options, averaged over 10 runs, and how many runs picked the right folder.

What we learned

Conclusions

  1. The folder's name matters more than the language. Luna finds the folder when its name is the word the user wrote: Bills for “phone bills”, 50/50. When the folder is called Facturas or Invoices it mostly says none fits (20/50 and 0/50), even in English.
  2. jev never picked a wrong folder, but its confidence shows the same pull. It gives Bills 100%. Invoices, a word the user never said, drops to 59%–71%, and climbs back to 89%–90% once the assistant's reply says “invoices”. The word moves jev's confidence, not its answer. In Spanish, where Facturas is the everyday word, it stays at 90%–98%.
  3. Without the folder's word, Luna struggles even with an obvious match. When the user says “phone service”, Luna gets Bills 40/50 and Invoices 0/50.
  4. The weakness is specific to Luna Decisions. The same GPT-6 Luna, asked as a text model, gets 245/250, and Claude Haiku 5.5 gets 244/250. Every miss of theirs is the Invoices folder going to Personal, the same cases where jev's confidence drops. They return no probabilities and are slower: a median of 1.3 s and 1.0 s per call, against 0.2 s for jev.
  5. The word “personal” is not the cause. Without it, Luna leans as much towards the Personal folder, and in one case more.
  6. For this feature, a folder suggestion should not depend on the user typing the folder's exact name. In this test, jev met it in every run. The text models missed it in 5 and 6 runs of 250.

Limits

  • One topic, hand-written messages, one wording of the question, three folders. It shows a pattern, not a general ranking of these models.
  • The text models get one prompt, written for this test, with the gateway's default settings. Another wording could change their numbers.
  • jev varies between runs, so a rerun will not give exactly these numbers.
  • The bare question (message B) ran on 2026-10-09 and the other messages on 2026-10-10, with the same models and settings.
  • experimental_decide is experimental in the AI SDK, and the Decisions API is in beta.
